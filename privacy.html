Privacy Policy

Anatomy Recall for iPhone and iPad · last updated 6 September 2026

Anatomy Recall collects nothing about you.

No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. Your study history never leaves your device unless you choose to add your own Claude API key for the tutor, and then only what is described below is sent, only to Anthropic, only under your own account.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app remembers, which is the structures you have learned, your review schedule, streak, XP, quiz results and settings, is written to a database on your device, in a container the app shares only with its own home-screen widget, which reads the same data to show your progress. It is not uploaded anywhere. It is included in your device backup if you back up your iPhone or iPad, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Network use

The app is fully offline. The 3D models, audio pronunciations and all anatomy content are bundled in the download, and nothing is fetched at runtime.

There is exactly one case where the app makes a network request:

  • The optional AI tutor. The tutor answers questions about the structures you are studying. On devices with Apple Intelligence it runs entirely on your device and nothing is transmitted. The app uses Apple Intelligence whenever it is available, and only uses a Claude API key when it is not.

    Nothing is ever sent anywhere unless you paste your own Claude API key into Settings. If you do, each tutor request goes to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com) to generate the answer, and carries:

    • your question, and the rest of that conversation with the tutor, both sides;
    • the name of the structure you asked about;
    • the names of structures you recently studied (up to 20) and recently answered wrong (up to 10), so the answer can be tailored to what you are working on;
    • whether the app is in simple or full mode.

    It does not include your name, email address, device identifiers, streak, XP or quiz scores. The key and the Anthropic account are your own, and Anthropic's handling of the request is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy. The key is stored in the app's settings on your device, which means it is included in your device backups, and it is sent nowhere except to Anthropic as part of those requests. Remove it from Settings and the app stops making network requests entirely.

Tapping a "Source: Wikipedia" link opens Safari, which is governed by Apple's and Wikimedia's own policies.

Things Apple runs

Two things you may see are Apple's, not the app's:

  • The rating prompt. After a study session that went well, the app may ask iOS to show Apple's standard rating sheet, at most once every 30 days. The sheet, and anything you enter in it, goes to Apple and the App Store under Apple's terms; the app never learns who rated or what they wrote.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, iOS may record a diagnostic report. It reaches the developer only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements), and it contains no study data.

Children

Anatomy Recall does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. There is no account, no messaging between users, and no user-generated content shared with anyone.

Your choices

  • Delete all study data at any time: Settings, then Reset all progress, and tap it again to confirm.
  • Remove the AI key at any time: Settings, then clear the Claude API key field.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored.
